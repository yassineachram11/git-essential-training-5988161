## 1. Refund policy (new)

**Returns**
For health and hygiene reasons, a pillow can only be returned if it is unopened, unused and still in its original sealed packaging. To return a sealed pillow, contact us within 7 days of delivery.

**Damaged, faulty or wrong item**
If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and its packaging. We will replace it free of charge, including delivery. This applies even if the packaging has been opened.

**How to start a return**
Message us on WhatsApp or call +961 78 953 771, or email shoppiloco@gmail.com, with your order number. We will confirm whether your item qualifies and arrange the pickup or drop-off with you.

**Return delivery costs**
If the item is damaged, faulty or wrong, we cover return delivery. For other returns of sealed items, the return delivery cost is paid by the customer.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and check the returned item, we will refund you within 5 business days. Refunds are sent by Whish Money, whether you paid by Whish Money or in cash on delivery.

**Exchanges**
To exchange a sealed item for a different pack, contact us and we will arrange it.

**Contact**
Pilo Shop, Beirut, Lebanon. Phone and WhatsApp: +961 78 953 771. Email: shoppiloco@gmail.com

## 2. Shipping policy (new)

**Where we deliver**
We deliver everywhere in Lebanon. We do not ship outside Lebanon at the moment.

**Delivery cost**
Delivery is free on every order.

**Delivery time**
Orders are delivered within 1–5 days, depending on your location. These times are estimates and may be longer during holidays or for reasons outside our control.

**Order confirmation**
After you order, we may call or message you on the phone number you gave us to confirm your order and delivery address. Please make sure your phone number is correct and reachable.

**Payment**
Cash on delivery: pay the courier when your order arrives. Whish Money: pay before we ship your order. We will send you the payment details.

**If you miss the delivery**
The courier will contact you to arrange a new time. If we cannot reach you after several attempts, the order may be cancelled.

**Questions about your delivery**
Phone and WhatsApp: +961 78 953 771. Email: shoppiloco@gmail.com
